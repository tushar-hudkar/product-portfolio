## The Problem

E-commerce brands rely on high-quality visual content to showcase and sell products across marketplaces, their own websites, and digital marketing channels. This creates a continuous demand for product assets, including white-background images, lifestyle imagery, model photography, product infographics, and marketplace-specific content.

From the customer's perspective, creating and managing these assets at scale is challenging. Brand owners and e-commerce sellers need to ensure that their product content meets marketplace requirements, maintains brand consistency, and supports launches across multiple sales channels.

At Dresma, however, the challenge extended beyond asset creation. Production workflows relied on a combination of cloud storage, team communication, and creative tools. Although the business had the capabilities to produce assets at scale, the processes for requesting, managing, producing, reviewing, and delivering them remained fragmented, requiring significant manual coordination and intervention.

## Objective

To democratize e-commerce content creation for sellers and brand owners by reducing the time, cost, and operational effort required to produce high-quality digital assets. The vision was to support the entire content creation journey, from product image retouching and lifestyle imagery to social media creatives, high-engagement infographics, and Amazon listing content.

<Callout>
  The goal was not simply to build another set of creative tools, but to design a unified product experience that connected customer intent, creative production, task management, quality review, and final asset delivery into one cohesive workflow.
</Callout>

## Pain Points: Core Problems Faced by Our Users

1. **Uncertainty about content:** Which content types will most effectively increase engagement and sales on each platform.
2. **Difficulty maintaining cohesive brand identity:** Due to various e-commerce sites and social media channels.
3. **High costs:** Producing high-quality model imagery and lifestyle assets is costly.
4. **Manual overhead:** High manual overhead in product catalog creation and in maintaining various product categories.
5. **Personalization:** Struggle to personalize content efficiently while ensuring scalability and cost control.

## Challenges

- The problem we are solving is not new, but it is highly complex due to the need for end-to-end execution, detailed information, and precise outputs.
- The nature of the business is becoming increasingly volatile, as clients have diverse goals to achieve at different scales and sizes.
- As we gained deeper insight into the market and customer needs, it became clear that tailor-made solutions and a personalized experience were essential to achieving success.
- It is quite challenging to integrate linear journeys in a way that achieves a cohesive goal without disrupting the existing user journey.
- Early adoption and solution validation were essential to demonstrating measurable business impact.

## System Map: Data + Workflow Flow

**Data-driven decision:** My data-driven approach included stakeholder interviews, collaborative brainstorming with product managers, and direct involvement in sales discussions and customer success–led product demos. This allowed me to validate assumptions, understand customer expectations, and align design decisions with business and user needs.

<Figure src="/work/saas-content-platform/system-map.webp" alt="System map of client management, project management, brand and moodboard setup, recommendation, quality assessment and pricing systems, with pickup, artist, QC and CS boards" caption="Client management, project management, brand and mood-board setup, recommendation, quality assessment and pricing systems." tone="dark" />

- **Pickup Board:** The manager monitors progress using the artist directory, allocates projects, and tracks execution through the project tracker.
- **Artists Board:** The artist selects a task, understands the project details, and proceeds according to the defined instructions.
- **QC Board:** Captures feedback, manages pending reviews, records success or rejection reasons, and maintains a continuous comment loop.
- **CS Board:** Concludes with final delivery and project handover, followed by reporting, customer feedback, and credit details confirmation.
