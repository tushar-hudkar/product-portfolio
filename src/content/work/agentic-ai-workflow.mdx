Design a chat-based agentic system with collaborative agents operating in shared context for a seamless user experience.

## Why Agentic AI Workflow?

As the existing user flow fragmented across multiple tools and services, the process became ambiguous and difficult to manage. Each new requirement increased interface complexity, making it harder to maintain a coherent user journey.

Over time, the product became a collection of solutions rather than a focused system, making it difficult to gather meaningful user feedback and evaluate the effectiveness of individual features.

An Agentic AI workflow provided a way to reframe the problem. By shifting from feature-driven interfaces to intent-driven conversations, the system could adapt to varied user needs while keeping the experience cohesive, scalable, and easier to iterate based on real user outcomes.

## Discovery

**The anchor point: Identifying Problems**

A small group of clients demonstrated high adoption of critical flows, which motivated us to rethink the system design.

This insight led to the design of an Agentic AI workflow:

## Product Vision

The vision is to make complex production workflows feel simple and human-centered. Design an agentic, chat-based system built on a multi-agent collaboration model, where specialized agents operate through a shared context to deliver a seamless, human-centered experience.

### Conversational Orchestration with Contextual Intelligence

**System Design Principles**

- The system uses an agentic orchestrator to translate open-ended user conversations into structured, repeatable workflows, while all production tasks run through deterministic pipelines to ensure predictability and scalability.
- Clear agent identities and well-defined roles ensure transparency and predictability, while shared system memory preserves product and brand context across conversations.

**User Experience Principles**

- At every stage, users remain in control, able to review and refine outputs, with the flexibility to make key decisions at critical points throughout the interaction.
- Users focus on expressing what they want to achieve, not how the system works. The experience remains predictable and controllable, while allowing safe, iterative exploration.

## What AI Agents Mean to Users?

From the user's perspective, agents behave like collaborative assistants that are coordinated, transparent, and guided by intent rather than automation, grounded in core AX principles of **Intent Recognition, Transparency, and Context Awareness.**

### Unifying linear AI pipelines through an agentic, conversational workflow

**Agent's roles and responsibilities**

- Understand brand identity, establish visual and tonal consistency
- Build a visual moodboard and contextual scene suggestions
- Marketplace optimization & content strategist

## Striving for Harmony

The system prioritizes conversational intent, contextual continuity, and controlled execution to balance flexibility, trust, and scalability.

- **Human Intent:** The experience respects the natural, spontaneous, and sometimes unpredictable nature of human behavior, allowing users to explore and express intent freely.
- **AI Intelligence:** AI's analytical and adaptive capabilities are applied thoughtfully to support human intent, enhancing outcomes without constraining, overriding, or diminishing user control.

| Design Trade-offs | Key Design Decisions |
| --- | --- |
| Users can express intent naturally, but open-ended input increases ambiguity. | Showing editable results first, rather than perfect initial accuracy, is needed for icebreaking. |
| Oversimplifying the conversation may look like a loss of control to users. | Keeping users in the loop intentionally whenever agents make important decisions builds trust. |
| In a real-time conversation, expecting high-value results may become a latency issue. | Visibility of agents is necessary to get the right intent from users and provide anticipated results. |

## Design for Collaboration

The conversation flow is designed around a multi-agent collaboration model, where specialized AI agents take ownership of specific tasks while sharing a unified understanding of brand, product, and marketplace constraints. The language and tone are intentionally friendly and empathetic, helping users feel at ease and motivated throughout the experience.

## Finding the Right Balance

Balancing customer needs, creative workflows, and system capabilities required careful consideration of how AI could fit naturally into existing processes. The experience evolved around making content production more structured and manageable, while exploring opportunities to reduce manual effort, improve workflow visibility, and support scalability.
